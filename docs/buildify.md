## RevenueCycle AI - MVP Development Plan

### Requirements

**Core Value Proposition:**
"Never lose a lead. Never forget a follow-up. Never overlook an overdue invoice."

**Primary Dashboard Features:**
1. Revenue metrics at a glance (Invoiced, Paid, Outstanding, Overdue)
2. AI Revenue Action Plan - Top 5 daily revenue opportunities
3. Follow-ups needed - Leads requiring attention
4. Overdue invoices - Payment recovery opportunities
5. Reactivation opportunities - Dormant customers ready to purchase
6. Hot leads - High-scoring leads ready to contact

**User Roles (Phase 8):**
- Business Owner (view all, manage everything)
- Sales Manager (manage leads, monitor pipeline)
- Sales Rep (view assigned leads, update status)
- Finance/Admin (invoices, payments, reminders)

### Designs

**MVP Dashboard:**
- Single-page responsive layout
- Sidebar navigation (8 main sections)
- Header with business name & settings
- Revenue metrics cards (5 columns, responsive)
- AI Action Plan card (hero section, priority-based)
- Two-column layout: Follow-ups + Overdue Invoices
- Reactivation opportunities grid
- Hot leads section
- Placeholder for future features

**Color Scheme:**
- Light theme (light bg, dark text)
- Primary: Brand blue
- Destructive: Red (for overdue, critical)
- Muted: Gray (secondary info)
- Urgency badges: Critical (red), High (orange), Medium (yellow), Normal (blue)
